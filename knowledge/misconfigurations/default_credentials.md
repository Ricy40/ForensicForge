# Default or weak credentials

Leaving a service, application, or OS account on its vendor-default
username/password pair (e.g. `admin`/`admin`), or setting an easily
guessable password (e.g. `password123`), is one of the most common
real-world initial-access vectors.

**Why it's a real vulnerability:** default credential lists for almost every
common device and application are publicly documented and built into
automated scanning and exploitation tools. No technical exploit is required
— only knowledge of the default, or a small dictionary of common weak
passwords.
